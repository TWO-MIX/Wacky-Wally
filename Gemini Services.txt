# Gemini AI & API Integration: AI Space Planner

This application leverages the Gemini API to transform static space planning into a dynamic, intelligent simulation. Below are the specific ways Gemini AI is utilized to enhance the user experience.

## 1. Computer Vision: Sketch-to-Simulation
**Model:** `gemini-3-flash-preview`
**Feature:** Users can upload a photo of a hand-drawn floor plan or a rough digital sketch.
**How it works:** Gemini Vision analyzes the image, identifies spatial elements (desks, meeting rooms, coffee stations, entrances, obstacles), and extracts their relative coordinates and labels.
**User Benefit:** Eliminates the need for manual CAD-style data entry. Organisers can go from a "napkin sketch" to a functional simulation in seconds.

## 2. Qualitative Analysis: The "Vibe Check"
**Model:** `gemini-3.1-pro-preview`
**Feature:** An "AI Insights" button that provides a critical review of the current layout.
**How it works:** The app sends the current floor plan data, occupancy stats, and agent counts to Gemini. The model identifies potential "dead zones," social bottlenecks, or inefficient staff routes.
**User Benefit:** Provides professional-grade spatial consulting instantly, helping organisers optimize for "vibe" and efficiency without needing a background in architecture.

## 3. Natural Language Simulation Tuning: Environmental Factors
**Model:** `gemini-3-flash-preview`
**Feature:** A "Vibe" input field that changes the global state of the simulation.
**How it works:** Users can type phrases like "Monday Morning Rush" or "Post-Lunch Slump." Gemini translates these abstract concepts into mathematical modifiers for agent movement speed, coffee desire, and social interaction probability.
**User Benefit:** Allows organisers to test "what-if" scenarios using natural language instead of complex slider configurations.

## 4. Persona Generation: Intelligent Agents
**Model:** `gemini-3-flash-preview`
**Feature:** Automated generation of diverse agent behaviors.
**How it works:** When agents are added to the simulation, Gemini generates unique metrics for each persona (Participant, Organiser, Staff) including their walking speed, desk-focus frequency, and social tendencies.
**User Benefit:** Creates a realistic, non-uniform crowd simulation that accurately reflects how different types of people use a space.

## 5. Scenario-Specific Behavior: Hackathon Behavior Engine
**Model:** `gemini-3.1-pro-preview`
**Feature:** A specialized behavior prompt for event-specific phases.
**How it works:** Users describe a specific moment (e.g., "The final 2 hours of the hackathon"). Gemini generates persona-specific multipliers (e.g., Participants get +50% Activity Level, Staff get +30% Movement Speed).
**User Benefit:** Enables hyper-specific stress-testing of a space for the most critical moments of an event, ensuring resources (like pizza stations or meeting rooms) are positioned correctly for peak demand.
